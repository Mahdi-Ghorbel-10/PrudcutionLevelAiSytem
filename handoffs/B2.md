## Substep 2: deviations

- The DOCX loader now keeps heading styles as `#` lines. The spec's chunker splits on headings, and without this Word files would have no heading path. The loader's text output changed.
- Overlap is added only when the overlap plus the next piece fits within the size limit. A piece near the full size gets no overlap, so the size limit always holds.
- Pieces inside a chunk are joined with a single newline, not the original spacing.

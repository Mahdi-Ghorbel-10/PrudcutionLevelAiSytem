## Deviations

- The DOCX fixture needed trimming to meet the spec's 20 KB limit. python-docx's default template is about 36 KB. The fixture drops the `stylesWithEffects.xml` part, the `latentStyles` block in `styles.xml`, and the thumbnail. The file still opens in python-docx with its Heading 1 style intact. Word itself was not tested.
- `Document.page` is `None` for txt, md and docx, since those formats have no pages. The spec said `page` without saying what it holds for non-paged formats.
- `load_file` takes a path and picks the loader by file suffix. The spec did not name the function.
